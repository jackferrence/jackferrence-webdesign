Common questions

Before you call

What does your prep actually include?

Pressure wash, scrape, sand, caulk every seam, patch cracks, and prime bare substrate. Prep is roughly half the labor on an exterior job and it's the entire reason a paint job lasts.

How long will exterior paint last?

A properly prepped exterior with premium coating runs seven to ten years in this climate. South and west exposures weather fastest.

Are you lead-safe certified?

Yes. EPA Lead-Safe RRP certification is legally required for work on homes built before 1978, and we carry it.

Do you help pick colors?

Yes, and we handle HOA color approval submissions when your association requires them.

How long does a house take?

Most exteriors run three to five days. Interiors are similar depending on square footage and how much drywall repair is involved.
