Common questions

Before you call

Do I get a plan before you start?

Always. You see a measured, drawn plan with a plant schedule and approve it before anything is dug. The design fee is credited toward the build.

Are there rebates for removing my lawn?

Usually yes, through California and local water district turf replacement programs. Eligibility depends on your district and square footage. We check first and file the paperwork for you.

How long does a project take?

A drought-tolerant conversion is typically two to three weeks. A full design-build with hardscape runs four to eight depending on scope and permitting.

Do you do ongoing maintenance?

Yes, on a monthly agreement after the install. Most clients keep us for the first year while plants establish.

Will drought-tolerant look like gravel and cactus?

Not the way we design it. Native and Mediterranean planting gives you real color and structure — it just doesn't need forty inches of water a year.
