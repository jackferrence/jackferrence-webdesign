Common questions

Before you call

How often should a pool be serviced?

Weekly is standard in Southern California. Chemistry moves fast in our heat, and a two-week gap is usually where algae starts.

Are chemicals included in the monthly price?

Yes. The monthly rate covers all standard chemicals. The only separate charges are equipment repairs and specialty treatments like a green pool recovery.

Do I need to be home for service?

No. I need gate access and a clear path to the equipment pad. You get a text with a photo when the visit is finished.

What if my equipment breaks?

I diagnose and repair pumps, filters, heaters, and salt cells. Diagnostic is $95 and gets credited toward the repair if you move forward.

How long does it take to fix a green pool?

Most are swimmable in three to five days with a shock treatment and heavy filtering. Severely neglected pools may need a drain and acid wash, which I'll quote before starting.
