Common questions

Before you call

Do you charge for an estimate?

Estimates are free for scheduled work. Emergency diagnostic calls carry a fee that comes off the repair if you move forward the same visit.

How fast can you get here?

Same day for anything actively leaking or backing up. Next day for everything else.

Do you pull the permit?

Yes. Water heater replacements, repipes, and sewer work all require permits, and we handle them.

Why is my water bill suddenly high?

Usually a running toilet or a slab leak. Shut off every fixture and watch the meter — if it's still moving, call us.

Repair or replace my water heater?

If it's leaking from the tank, replace. If it's not heating, it's often a $200 part. We diagnose before quoting a replacement.
