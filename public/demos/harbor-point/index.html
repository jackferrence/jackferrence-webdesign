Common questions

Before you call

Do you come to the boat, or does it need to be hauled?

I work at the slip for most jobs. Only haul-out work — running gear, shafts, hull penetrations — needs the yard, and I'll say so before we schedule.

Which marinas can you work at?

I'm an approved vendor at Balboa Yacht Basin, Lido Marina Village, and Newport Dunes, and I carry the insurance most Newport Harbour marinas require.

How large a vessel do you service?

Power and sail up to about 65 feet. Larger vessels case by case.

Do you handle emergency calls?

Yes, for existing customers and when I'm in the harbor. Call rather than email if it's urgent.

What does a service call cost?

$165 an hour with a two-hour minimum, which covers travel to the slip. Parts are quoted separately before ordering.
