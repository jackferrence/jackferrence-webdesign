Common questions

Before you call

Do I need a permit to replace my AC?

Yes. California requires a permit and HERS testing on system changeouts. We pull the permit and schedule the HERS rater as part of the job.

What is HERS testing?

An independent verification that your ducts and refrigerant charge meet Title 24 standards. It's required on most changeouts and it's included in our replacement pricing.

Is the diagnostic fee credited if I do the repair?

Yes. The $89 fee comes off the repair price if you move forward the same visit.

Are there rebates for heat pumps?

Often, through California and utility programs. Eligibility depends on your equipment and home. We check before quoting and file the paperwork for you.

How long does a system replacement take?

Most homes are done in one day. Older homes needing duct or electrical work can run two.
